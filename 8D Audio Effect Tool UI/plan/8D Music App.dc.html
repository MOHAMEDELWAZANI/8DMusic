Real-time 8D sound for everything the phone plays

8D Music takes audio that is already playing and moves it on a path around the listener's head, live, on the device — no uploads, no re-encoded files, no waiting. The design problem is not the effect; it is that Android gives three different ways to reach the audio, each with a different cost. The whole interface is built to make that choice honest and reversible.

1 · DIRECT CAPTURE

Spatialises whatever app is playing through Android's playback capture. Nothing to set up — but apps are allowed to refuse it, and some do. The design treats that refusal as a first-class state (screen 2i), not an error.

2 · LOCAL PLAYER

The route that always works, online or not. Files from the device, playlists the user builds, and each playlist able to carry its own preset so the effect changes with the listening. This is the home of the app, not a fallback.

3 · SYSTEM-WIDE (SHIZUKU)

Shell-level access granted through Shizuku, no root. Every app, including the ones that block capture. It costs one careful pairing, so the setup is a live checklist that reacts to the phone's real state rather than a slideshow.

GUIDE · SHIZUKU · 1 OF 3

Why this step exists

Android won't let one app touch another app's audio unless it's given permission from a higher level. Shizuku is what grants that, without rooting the phone. It's the only way to get 8D on apps that block capture.

WITHOUT Works on the apps that allow capture, plus your local files

2h About story + replay guides
ABOUT

Why I built 8D Music

I kept hearing 8D edits online and wanted the effect on my own music — not a re-uploaded version of someone else's mix. Every tool I found was a website that made you upload a file and wait.

So this does it live, on the phone, on whatever is already playing. No uploads, no accounts required, no waiting. Different sources, same 8D experience — simple for everyone, and open for anyone who wants to go deeper.
