8D MUSIC - PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING

8D Music makes sound seem to move around your head, on everything your computer
plays - Spotify, YouTube, games, films. Use headphones; the effect is built on
the difference between what your left and right ear hear.

Three things you should know first.


1. THIS NEEDS ADMINISTRATOR ACCESS.

   The effect installs into Windows' own audio system, and Windows will not let
   an ordinary program do that.


2. YOU WILL NEED TO RESTART YOUR COMPUTER.

   Windows only picks up a new audio effect properly after a restart. Setup
   will offer to restart for you when it finishes.


3. IT CHANGES A WINDOWS SECURITY SETTING.

   This is the important one.

   Windows normally refuses to load audio effects unless Microsoft has signed
   them. 8D Music is not signed, so Setup switches that check off. The setting
   is called DisableProtectedAudioDG.

   What that means for you:

     - It applies to the whole computer, not only to 8D Music. Any other
       unsigned audio effect could load too.

     - Some copy-protected music and video - certain streaming services, some
       DVD and Blu-ray players - may refuse to play, or play without sound,
       while it is off. That is the protected content noticing the check is
       disabled. Nothing is damaged, and it stops when you uninstall.

   Uninstalling puts the setting back exactly as it was found. If some other
   audio software had already turned it off before you installed 8D Music, the
   uninstaller leaves it off rather than breaking that software.

   If you are not comfortable with this, close Setup now. Nothing here is
   hidden from you, and that is deliberate.


WHAT ELSE IT DOES

   It does not change your output device. Whatever you had selected stays
   selected.

   It records every setting it changes in
       C:\ProgramData\8DMusic\install-backup.ini
   and the uninstaller restores from that file.
