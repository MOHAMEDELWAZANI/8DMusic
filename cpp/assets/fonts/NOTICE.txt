Typefaces bundled with 8D Music
===============================

Figtree.ttf
    SIL Open Font License 1.1 -- see OFL-Figtree.txt.
    Used for the whole interface.

PixelifySans-Bold.ttf
    SIL Open Font License 1.1 -- see OFL-PixelifySans.txt.
    Copyright 2021 The Pixelify Sans Project Authors.
    https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans
    Used for the Latin initials on the now-playing cover mark.
    Cut from the variable original at weight 700, so nothing here depends on
    variable-axis support: GDI on Windows has none.

KOMethlama-Medium.otf
    "Copyright (c) 2025 by HEY PORTER! / Tawfiq Dawi."
    Distributed by arfonts.net as free for personal and commercial use:
    https://www.arfonts.net/ko-methlama-medium-font
    Used for Arabic -- the cover mark's initials and any Arabic text in the
    interface, neither of which Figtree or Pixelify Sans can set.

The two mark faces cover disjoint alphabets: Pixelify Sans has all 52 Latin
letters and no Arabic, KO Methlama has 112 Arabic letters and no Latin.  That is
why they can sit in one family list and let the text renderer choose per word.
